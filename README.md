# 🏥 Smart Hospital Management System

A Smart Hospital Management System designed to simplify and automate hospital operations through a centralized digital platform.

## 📌 Project Overview

The Smart Hospital Management System provides a single platform for managing hospital-related activities such as patient records, doctors, appointments, departments, medical information, and hospital administration.

The main goal of this project is to reduce manual work, improve data management, and provide an efficient and user-friendly hospital management solution.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Manage patient information digitally.
- Manage doctor and staff information.
- Schedule and manage appointments.
- Maintain patient medical records.
- Manage hospital departments.
- Reduce paperwork and manual processes.
- Improve accessibility and organization of hospital data.
- Provide a centralized hospital management platform.

## 🚀 Features

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Management
- Add and manage doctor information.
- Manage doctor availability.
- Associate doctors with departments.

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Patient Management
- Register new patients.
- Store patient details.
- Maintain patient medical records.
- Update and retrieve patient information.

### 📅 Appointment Management
- Schedule appointments.
- Manage appointment details.
- Track doctor-patient appointments.

### 🏥 Department Management
- Manage hospital departments.
- Associate doctors and patients with departments.

### 📋 Medical Records
- Store patient medical history.
- Maintain diagnosis and treatment information.
- Provide organized access to medical records.
